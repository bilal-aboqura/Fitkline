# Fitkline

## Product

Fitkline is an Arabic-first commerce and lead-generation website for three sports-facility care products: STERIFIT, SHINYFIT, and FITMIST. The public site helps gym, club, studio, and sports-facility operators understand the role of each product, choose a 4 kg or 20 kg size, and place an order or request a quote.

## Users

- Public customers in Egypt comparing products and preparing an order.
- Fitkline staff managing copy, product images, sizes, prices, availability, orders, and payment readiness.

## Surfaces

- Public brand and commerce website: premium sports-industrial, Arabic RTL, black/graphite surfaces, Fitkline neon green, strong product photography.
- Admin dashboard: restrained, task-focused product UI with clear tables, forms, status states, and responsive navigation.

## Business rules

- The active catalog contains only 4 kg and 20 kg variants.
- Prices may be unpublished; the UI must not invent a price.
- Kashier online payment is offered only when every ordered item has a configured price and server credentials are present.
- Card details never touch or persist in Fitkline. Customers pay on Kashier's hosted session.
- Shipping and availability remain subject to the configured operational settings.

## Design direction

- Public brand voice: powerful, mechanical, disciplined.
- Admin voice: clear, calm, operational.
- Existing identity is preserved: Alexandria/Lalezar, dark surfaces, and the Fitkline green accent.
- Product imagery must always be fully framed with no clipping.
